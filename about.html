Our story

A cooperative built by women, for women — keeping old crafts alive and opening our home to the world.

Who we are

Women, together, making things that last

Coopérative Féminine Ouizelne was born from a simple idea: that the skills our mothers and grandmothers carried — pressing argan, grinding grain on stone, weaving with our hands — deserve to be valued, protected and passed on.

We are a group of women from the same land, sharing the work and the reward fairly. Together we grow and gather, grind and press, weave and paint. Nothing leaves our hands without care. And nothing is sold without the story behind it.

Today, more than ever, we welcome visitors to sit with us, share a meal and learn — because our culture is not a display, it is our daily life, and we are happy to share it.

Our journey

From a few pairs of hands to a shared home

A slow, honest path — built one season at a time.

A small beginning

A handful of women began meeting to press argan oil and grind grain the way they had learned as girls.

Becoming a cooperative

We organised ourselves formally so that every woman would earn fairly and share in the decisions.

Opening our doors

Visitors started asking to see how it was done — so we set a table and poured the tea.

Reaching the world

With 14.7K+ friends online, our craft now travels far — while our roots stay deep at home.
