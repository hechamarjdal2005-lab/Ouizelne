How the day goes

Slow, warm and hands-on

A gentle rhythm — adjust it and make it your own. Every visit is a little different.

🌿 Suitable for all ages 📸 Photos welcome 🧕 Women-led

Welcome & mint tea

You arrive, you are seated, and the tea is poured. Introductions and a little conversation.

Craft demonstration

We show you how argan is cracked, ground and pressed — and how things are woven by hand.

Hands-on workshop

Join in: turn the grinding stone, shape the dough, or try the loom with us.

The shared meal

Tagine, fresh bread and seasonal dishes — cooked and eaten together at one table.

Tea, talk & farewell

More tea, questions, photos, and a small gift from our kitchen before you go.

Good to know

Before you come

🕐

When to visit

Any day, best in the late morning or late afternoon. We will agree on a time together.

🥗

Dietary needs

Vegetarian, vegan and allergies are easy to accommodate — just tell us in advance.

🚗

Getting there

We will send exact directions on WhatsApp. Transport can often be arranged.

👟

Comfort

Bring comfy shoes and modest clothing. Everything else is waiting for you.

💳

Payment

Cash on the day, or arrange it with us in advance over WhatsApp.

🌧️

Weather

We are mostly outdoors — in colder months we gather around the fire inside.
